# Health-data experiment example

This directory contains reproducible experiment code, data-source recipes,
protocol definitions, numerical results, result figures and table fragments.
It does not distribute the manuscript, supplementary article or reviewer letters.
The source experiment archive was version 1.4.15; this GitHub selection excludes
its supplementary article PDF/TeX and article bibliography.

- Primary protocol and results: primary-protocol/README_primary_protocol.md.
- Additional experiments: REPRODUCE.md and reproduction/benchmark/README.md.
- Public-data recipes: DATA_SOURCES.md and data-source-files.csv.
- External experiments: institutional/external-validation-20261017/README_reproduction.txt.
- Experimental figures and table fragments: supplement/figures/ and supplement/tables/.

Raw health records, patient-level prediction arrays and trained models remain local.
