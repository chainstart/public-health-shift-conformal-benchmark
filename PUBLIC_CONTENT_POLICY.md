# Public repository content policy

Distribute benchmark code, dependencies, data-acquisition recipes, method examples,
evaluation protocols, numerical results, experimental figures/table fragments,
and instructions needed to reproduce and extend the benchmark.

The author explicitly authorized public distribution of the supplementary article
PDF on 2026-10-24; its approved copy is in `docs/supplementary/`.

Do not distribute the submitted or planned-submission main article text,
full LaTeX sources, graphical/textual abstract submissions,
editorial correspondence, reviewer comments or response letters. Keep those
materials in the private author workspace. Table fragments and experimental
plots are not complete article documents and may remain as benchmark evidence.

Publication of benchmark code or results does not authorize publication of the
article. Scan the tracked inventory and release contents before every release.
